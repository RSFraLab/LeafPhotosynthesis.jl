Stomatal Conductance, Gas Diffusion, Equilibrium & Oxygen ​

Gas-exchange components separate stomatal target laws (BallBerry, Medlyn) and time-evolution semantics (SteadyStateStomata, PrognosticStomata, NoStomatalDynamics) from boundary+stomatal series diffusion (SeriesDiffusion, WaterVaporDiffusion), safeguarded scalar equilibrium root-finding (Bisection, SafeguardedNewton, EquilibriumSolver), analytic fixed-temperature gas-exchange sensitivities, and explicit gross/net O₂ budgets (CoreOxygenExchange, NoOxygenExchange).

Walkthroughs: Core exchange, Equilibrium & sensitivities, Oxygen response.

Medlyn(minimum_conductance, slope, micromole_to_mole, vpd_floor)

Parameterize the Medlyn stomatal H₂O conductance target    [mol H₂O m⁻² leaf s⁻¹], where   is the leaf-surface CO₂ mole fraction (surface_co2 / pressure in StomatalDrivers), with minimum_conductance (g₀), slope (g₁ [Pa¹ᐟ²]), micromole_to_mole [mol μmol⁻¹] converting A_n, and positive regularization vpd_floor (D_min [Pa]).

LeafPhotosynthesis.target_conductance Function
julia
target_conductance(target, drivers::StomatalDrivers, diffusion::SeriesDiffusion)
target_conductance(stomatal_model, drivers::StomatalDrivers, diffusion::SeriesDiffusion)

Evaluate the steady-state stomatal H₂O conductance target [mol H₂O m⁻² leaf s⁻¹] of a BallBerry or Medlyn target (directly, in a SteadyStateStomata or PrognosticStomata, or in a LeafModel) for drivers, or return nothing for NoStomatalDynamics.

diffusion is the leaf's CO₂ diffusion path; its diffusivity_ratio is the H₂O/CO₂ ratio of the Medlyn law (the one value the stomatal law and the diffusion share). Ball–Berry does not use it (its slope already includes the ratio).

Each law checks only the driver it uses; the other may be NaN. Net assimilation ≤ 0 gives the minimum conductance g₀.

LeafPhotosynthesis.SeriesDiffusion Type
julia
SeriesDiffusion{FT}(; boundary_co2, mesophyll_co2, diffusivity_ratio, mole_to_micromole)
SeriesDiffusion(boundary_co2, mesophyll_co2, diffusivity_ratio, mole_to_micromole)

Parameterize series CO₂ transport across boundary-layer conductance boundary_co2 [mol CO₂ m⁻² leaf s⁻¹], stomatal H₂O conductance (via StomatalState divided by H₂O/CO₂ diffusivity_ratio), and mesophyll conductance mesophyll_co2 [mol CO₂ m⁻² leaf s⁻¹], with unit factor mole_to_micromole [μmol mol⁻¹].

diffusivity_ratio is the only place the package stores the H₂O/CO₂ ratio of stomatal conductances (1.6 for molecular diffusion in air; there is no default): the Medlyn target reads it from the diffusion passed to target_conductance, leaf_tendencies and equilibrium_gas_exchange.

Passing Inf for boundary_co2 or mesophyll_co2 removes that resistance. The conductances may vary from leaf to leaf (wind, leaf size), so any value is stored without throwing; gas_exchange, assimilation_sensitivity and equilibrium_gas_exchange share one domain: boundary_co2 > 0 and mesophyll_co2 > 0 (Inf allowed), and finite positive diffusivity_ratio and mole_to_micromole. Outside it they return NaN (EquilibriumInvalidInput).

LeafPhotosynthesis.gas_exchange Function
julia
gas_exchange(photosynthesis, environment, state, diffusion,
    excitation=BroadbandExcitation())
gas_exchange(leaf, environment, state, diffusion, excitation=BroadbandExcitation())

Solve analytical steady-state CO₂ diffusion and C3 assimilation at prescribed nonnegative stomatal conductance state (Liu et al. 2024, Eqs. 1–2).

Supports MinimumLimitation with SignedAssimilation (for both C3Photosynthesis and CytochromePhotosynthesis) without A–Ci iteration: each limiting branch      meets the supply   at the root of a quadratic, and the binding branch is the larger root above the compensation point and the smaller one below it. Return the fields of photosynthesize (the carbon fluxes of carbon_assimilation, electron_transport and the model's extra electron diagnostics) at the solved biochemical_co2, followed by surface_co2, intercellular_co2, biochemical_co2 [Pa], total CO₂ conductance [mol m⁻² leaf s⁻¹] and diffusion_residual [μmol CO₂ m⁻² leaf s⁻¹]. Invalid excitation or forcing gives NaN in every field (see is_valid).

Closed stomata (state.conductance = 0): the total conductance g is zero and each branch's quadratic reduces to    . When   for both branches, the result is the steady state of a sealed leaf in light: biochemical_co2 is the CO₂ compensation point of the model, respiration included,    , net assimilation is zero (up to rounding; diffusion_residual reports it), intercellular_co2 equals biochemical_co2 and surface_co2 equals ambient CO₂. When a branch has   (darkness, or light below the light compensation point) no steady state exists, since respiration would accumulate CO₂ in the sealed leaf without bound, and every field is NaN.

LeafPhotosynthesis.leaf_tendencies Function
julia
leaf_tendencies(leaf, environment, stomatal_state, diffusion,
    excitation=BroadbandExcitation())

Evaluate prescribed-conductance gas_exchange, the stomatal target and tendency, and the oxygen_exchange budget at stomatal_state without mutating state.

leaf.stomata is a PrognosticStomata or NoStomatalDynamics; a SteadyStateStomata leaf has no tendency (solve it with equilibrium_gas_exchange) and is a MethodError.

Return (; gas_exchange, conductance_target, conductance_tendency, oxygen):

  • gas_exchange: the gas_exchange result at stomatal_state;

  • conductance_target [mol H₂O m⁻² leaf s⁻¹] and conductance_tendency [mol H₂O m⁻² leaf s⁻²] of target_conductance and stomatal_tendency, with drivers from the gas exchange (net assimilation, surface CO₂) and environment (pressure, relative humidity, VPD) and the diffusivity ratio of diffusion; for NoStomatalDynamics the target is the state conductance and the tendency zero;

  • oxygen: the coupled oxygen_exchange at the solved biochemical_co2, or nothing for NoOxygenExchange.

Each part follows its own domain: inputs outside the gas-exchange domain give NaN in every field of gas_exchange and oxygen and, through the drivers, in the outputs of PrognosticStomata; invalid stomatal drivers alone (for example relative humidity outside [0, 1] with a Ball–Berry target, or a NaN VPD with a Medlyn target) give NaN stomatal outputs only. The humidity driver the target does not read may be NaN.

LeafPhotosynthesis.EquilibriumStatus Type
julia
EquilibriumStatus

Enumerate non-throwing outcome codes returned by equilibrium_gas_exchange:

  • EquilibriumConverged: both pressure and flux residuals satisfy EquilibriumSolver tolerances.

  • EquilibriumIterationLimit: max_iterations reached before meeting tolerances.

  • EquilibriumPrecisionLimit: floating-point bracket width exhausted before meeting tolerances.

  • EquilibriumInvalidInput: non-finite or out-of-domain parameters or environment inputs.

  • EquilibriumInvalidBracket: physical CO₂ bracket failed to straddle a finite root, or no steady state exists (net respiration with zero minimum conductance).

  • EquilibriumStomataClosed: zero minimum conductance and no open-stomata root; the solution is the closed-stomata limit at the CO₂ compensation point.

LeafPhotosynthesis.equilibrium_gas_exchange Function
julia
equilibrium_gas_exchange(photosynthesis, SteadyStateStomata(target), environment,
    diffusion, solver, excitation=BroadbandExcitation(), initial_co2=nothing)
equilibrium_gas_exchange(leaf, environment, diffusion, solver,
    excitation=BroadbandExcitation(), initial_co2=nothing)

Solve coupled steady-state C3 assimilation (C3Photosynthesis or CytochromePhotosynthesis), series CO₂ diffusion, and Ball–Berry or Medlyn stomatal conductance at prescribed leaf temperature and humidity.

biochemical_co2 (Cc) is solved inside a physics-derived bracket while intercellular_co2 (Ci) and surface_co2 (Cs) are reconstructed from mesophyll and boundary-layer conductances. Thermal kinetic rates and available electron supply are evaluated once per solve, darkness uses an exact analytic branch, and invalid inputs or non-convergence return explicit EquilibriumStatus codes without throwing.

The target law    accepts a zero minimum conductance g₀ (common in Medlyn fits). With   the closed state is a steady state whenever Γ exists, so it can coexist with an open root; the solver then returns the open root, because the result is defined as the limit   of the unique solution for   and so does not jump when a fitted g₀ approaches zero:

  • if net assimilation is negative at every CO₂ (darkness, or light below the light compensation point), no steady state exists: EquilibriumInvalidBracket, NaN fields;

  • otherwise the model's CO₂ compensation point Γ (respiration included) exists. If     , stomata are open at the solution, which is the root in found as for   (EquilibriumConverged);

  • otherwise the solution is the closed-stomata limit: stomatal and total conductance zero, net assimilation zero to the flux tolerance and biochemical_co2 = Γ (EquilibriumStomataClosed, valid). This includes ambient CO₂ at or below Γ.

initial_co2 [Pa] warm-starts the iteration, typically from the previous time step's biochemical_co2. It is nothing (start at the midpoint of the CO₂ bracket) or a scalar of the model's binary precision (a dual number over it counts by its value); another number type, including an integer, is a MethodError, as for every other argument. A warm start that is not finite or lies outside the open bracket falls back to the midpoint. It is not used when  , in darkness, or when a bracket end already satisfies the tolerances. Being positional, it broadcasts: equilibrium_gas_exchange.(leaf, environments, diffusion, solver, BroadbandExcitation(), previous_co2) warm-starts every leaf from its own value. Return an EquilibriumSolution.

The humidity driver of the environment is checked per law: Ball–Berry needs a relative humidity in [0, 1] and ignores the VPD, Medlyn needs a finite VPD and ignores the relative humidity (the unused one may be NaN). The H₂O/CO₂ ratio of Medlyn is diffusion.diffusivity_ratio, the same value that converts stomatal H₂O conductance to CO₂ conductance in the diffusion path.

LeafPhotosynthesis.oxygen_exchange Function
julia
oxygen_exchange(oxygen_model, inputs::OxygenFluxInputs)
oxygen_exchange(oxygen_model, photosynthesis, environment,
    excitation=BroadbandExcitation())
oxygen_exchange(leaf, environment, excitation=BroadbandExcitation())

Return (; gross_production, photorespiratory_uptake, respiratory_uptake, net_release) [μmol O₂ m⁻² leaf s⁻¹]: gross O₂ evolution, photorespiratory and mitochondrial respiratory O₂ uptake, and net O₂ release (nothing for NoOxygenExchange). The coupled forms append the realized carboxylation and rubisco_oxygenation [μmol m⁻² leaf s⁻¹] and the realized linear_electron_flow [μmol e⁻ m⁻² leaf s⁻¹]; CytochromePhotosynthesis adds psi_electron_flow and cyclic_electron_flow. Input outside the physical domain gives NaN in every field.

Both forms compute the budget from carboxylation and oxygenation : with OxygenFluxInputs the caller gives them; when coupled to a signed C3Photosynthesis or CytochromePhotosynthesis model they are the realized turnover ( = photorespiratory CO₂ / co2_per_oxygenation), evaluated prior to net carbon cancellation so the compensation point causes no 0/0 singularity. The same fluxes give the same budget, bit for bit, in both forms. Gross O₂ evolution is the O₂ whose reductant the carbon cycles consume:   , one O₂ per CO₂ reduced to carbohydrate and, per oxygenation, the O₂ taken up (oxygen_per_oxygenation, ) less the CO₂ released (co2_per_oxygenation, ) by the photorespiratory cycle; with the canonical 1.5 and 0.5 this is   (two NADPH per carboxylation and per oxygenation, Johnson & Berry 2021, Eqs. 7–9). It does not depend on the electron stoichiometry of the photosynthesis model: electrons beyond the NADPH demand (an ATP-limited FvCB stoichiometry such as  ) are taken to return to O₂ without net O₂ exchange. Net O₂ release is therefore   , equal to net CO₂ uptake when respiratory_quotient is one. The coupled forms still report the realized linear_electron_flow.
