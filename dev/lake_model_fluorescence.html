LakeModelFluorescence is a fluorescence component like RateCompetitionFluorescence and CytochromeFluorescence: the same fluorescence call evaluates it, and it accepts the inputs that fluorescence_inputs returns for CytochromePhotosynthesis.

The comparison does not establish that the hypotheses of CytochromeFluorescence describe these leaves: its row carries chosen biochemical parameters, a prescribed non-stomatal drought schedule and a PSI detector weight transferred from another instrument and species (see the chosen inputs). It shows that the lake relations need treatment-specific or NPQ, or an equivalent electron flow, and that the result is sensitive to connectivity and to the observation model. A discriminating test needs the PAM record of each treatment ( with , NPQ and electron transport rate against PPFD) with matched gas exchange; Flexas et al. measured these, but they are not digitized here. Sustained quenching or photoinhibition under drought, which would lower , is held fixed in every row (Flexas et al. report dark of 0.80–0.83 across the leaves).
