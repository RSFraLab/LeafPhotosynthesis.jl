4. Scope limits ​

  • Internal photon fluxes (psii_photon_flux, psi_photon_flux, total_photon_flux) represent emission before within-leaf chlorophyll reabsorption, escape probabilities, and PSII/PSI spectral shaping (CanopyOptics.jl). Within-leaf chlorophyll reabsorption attenuates ~685 nm PSII fluorescence much more strongly than >710 nm PSI emission, increasing PSI's fractional contribution to escaped far-red SIF relative to pre-escape total_fluorescence_yield.

  • Quenching is steady-state feedback without an empirical upper PsbS/zeaxanthin ceiling or slow induction/relaxation memory. Lit states with zero biochemical turnover (Vcmax = 0 where  , or Vqmax = 0 where    is indeterminate) and PSII-starved far-red shade (  ) lie outside the steady-state   inversion domain and return FluorescenceIncompatibleRates.

  • Yields close per-photosystem photon excitation budgets ( ), not leaf sensible/latent/radiative joule budgets.
