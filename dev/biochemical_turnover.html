Electron use and biochemical turnover ​

biochemical_turnover(leaf, environment) exposes carboxylation, photorespiratory CO₂ release, respiration, realized linear electron flow, and available electron supply. It works with NoOxygenExchange(); requesting these diagnostics does not enable an oxygen calculation.

Run biochemical_turnover.jl to sweep prescribed biochemical-site CO₂ at fixed light and temperature. Its setup file handles parameter reading and assembly. The sweep settings, biochemistry, and core component choices are explicit. This is an illustrative diagnostic, not an additional Bonan exercise.

sh
julia --project=. examples/biochemical_turnover.jl
julia --project=docs docs/make.jl

Both plots offer both precisions, complete tables, and CSV/PNG downloads; the example gallery shows them next to the other examples. The calculation prescribes CO₂ at the biochemical site; it does not solve stomatal or mesophyll diffusion.

Equations and units ​

Let C and O be biochemical-site CO₂ and O₂ partial pressures [Pa], Γ* the photorespiratory compensation point [Pa], J the available electron supply [μmol electrons m⁻² leaf s⁻¹], and e and a the explicitly supplied electrons_per_carboxylation and oxygenation_cost. The existing model combines the two positive capacities

u_c = Vcmax / (C + Kc (1 + O/Ko))
u_j = J / (e (C + a Γ*))
u   = colimit(u_c, u_j)

using its selected minimum or quadratic limitation. The diagnostic uses that same capacity, without dividing carbon uptake by an efficiency:

carboxylation          = u C
photorespiratory_co2   = u Γ*
net_assimilation      = u (C − Γ*) − Rd
linear_electron_flow  = e u (C + a Γ*)
electron_transport    = J

Carbon fluxes and Rd are μmol CO₂ m⁻² leaf s⁻¹; electron fluxes are μmol electrons m⁻² leaf s⁻¹. At C = Γ*, the two gross carbon fluxes cancel, but electron flow can remain positive. Net assimilation there is −Rd, not zero. At darkness J = 0, realized flow is zero. At excess light, realized flow can be smaller than available supply. The oxygen budget reuses this turnover, converting photorespiratory CO₂ to oxygenation with its explicit stoichiometry.

This contract applies to SignedAssimilation. Clipping sub-compensation carbon uptake does not define a consistent gross turnover, so the clipped Bonan teaching variant has no method. Inputs must be finite and nonnegative, biochemical denominators positive, and Γ* consistent with O₂. The singular all-zero CO₂/Γ* denominator is outside this contract; it is not hidden with a floor.

Role in fluorescence coupling ​

The diagnostic supplies one input to a future fluorescence physiology component. It is not a fluorescence yield, an NPQ parameterization, or a photosystem model. Linear electron flow is assumed to serve carboxylation and photorespiration only; alternative electron sinks, cyclic flow, and oxygen limitation of respiration are absent. Turning unused electron capacity directly into fluorescence would require additional physical assumptions and is not implemented.

Physiology should supply a consistently normalized emitted photon source. Pigment absorption, leaf internal reabsorption and escape, directional canopy transport, and atmospheric transfer require separate interfaces. Their area and spectral conventions must prevent applying absorption, yield, or escape twice.

Validation ​

Turnover tests compare against an independent BigFloat electron-demand formulation for both limitations and Float32/Float64. They cover darkness, low light, zero CO₂ with positive Γ_, points on both sides of Γ_, exact compensation, carbon/O₂ consistency, inferred types, and allocation-free scalar evaluation. Mixed dark/lit batches run in both orders on CPU and NVIDIA CUDA, including NoOxygenExchange and disabled scalar GPU indexing. These checks test the stated biochemical closure; they do not validate a fluorescence model or Apple GPU execution.
