04 / MATHEMATICAL STRUCTURE & SCOREBOARD

Three equations explain every curve above

1. Single-variable \(K_N(x)\) peak invariance

Empirical rate-competition models parameterize regulated heat dissipation solely as a function of relative PSII light saturation \(x = 1 - \Phi_P / \Phi_{P0} \in [0, 1]\):

\[ \frac{\Phi_F(x)}{\Phi_{F,o}} = \frac{1 - \Phi_{P0}(1 - x)}{(1 - \Phi_{P0})\left[1 + \dfrac{K_N(x)}{K_F + K_D}\right]} \]

Lowering \(g_{sw}\) or \(V_{c\max}\) rescales where \(x(\mathrm{PPFD})\) lies along the irradiance axis, but every sweep traverses the exact same scalar curve, locking global peak height at \(1.462\) (\(x^* \approx 0.19\), Cotton) or \(1.453\) (\(x^* \approx 0.23\), Drought) at every conductance.

2. Two-state Cyt \(b_6f\) + PAM-2000 baseline

Johnson & Berry (2021) decouple open PSII trap fraction \(q_2(Q_1, V_{q\max})\) from stromal sink yield \(\Phi_{P2}(Q_2, C_c, V_{c\max})\), observed through long-pass (\(>700\;\mathrm{nm}\)) PSI/PSII weights \(\varepsilon_{F1}/\varepsilon_{F2} = 2.0\):

\[ \frac{F_s}{F_o} = \frac{\varepsilon_{F2}\,\alpha_2\,\Phi_{F2} + \varepsilon_{F1}\,\alpha_1\,\Phi_{F1}} {\varepsilon_{F2}\,\alpha_{2,\mathrm{d}}\,\Phi_{F2,o} + \varepsilon_{F1}\,\alpha_{1,\mathrm{d}}\,\Phi_{F1,o}} \]

Redox-invariant PSI emission (\(\sim 39\%\) of dark \(F_o\)) yields dark apparent \(F_v / F_m = \mathbf{0.821}\) (measured: \(0.80\text{–}0.83\)), while earlier \(A_j \to A_c\) crossover under drought truncates the \(q_2\)-driven rise at progressively lower \(F_s / F_o\).

3. Photorespiratory electron-sink floor

In ambient air (\(O = 21\;\mathrm{kPa}\)), even at \(C_c = \Gamma_*\) where net carboxylation and photorespiration cancel in carbon uptake, gross oxygenation and cyclic PSI flow (\(\eta(\Gamma_*) = 13/12 \approx 1.083\)) keep consuming electrons:

\[ J_{P700,c}(\Gamma_*) = \frac{13\,\Gamma_*\,V_{c\max}}{\Gamma_* + K_c(1 + O/K_o)} \]

At \(25\;^\circ\mathrm{C}\), \(J_{P700,c}(\Gamma_*) \approx 0.799\,V_{c\max}\): for \(V_{c\max} = 96\), pure stomatal closure cannot push \(J_{P700,c}\) below \(76.7\;\mu\mathrm{mol\;e^-\;m^{-2}\;s^{-1}}\) (\(\approx 79.3\) as \(g_{sw} \to 0\) with \(R_d = 0.80\)), explaining why severe grapevine drought (\(g_{sw} < 60\)) reflects coordinated non-stomatal downregulation.
